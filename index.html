Who we are

Three years making cannabis products for other companies. Now for ourselves.

Sophia’s Garden was built as a contract manufacturer. Since 2022 we have produced more than thirty cannabis SKUs for other licence holders and delivered over a million units into Ontario and four other provinces. That work is still part of the business.

Our work sits where confectionery craft meets cannabinoid formulation. We develop cocoa butter cannabinoid extracts, build them into chocolate so that dose is even and stable, and manufacture in small batches at our own facility. The extract side runs on the same discipline in a different carrier.

Questions

Common questions

What does Sophia’s Garden Artisan make?

Precision-dosed cannabis products. We are a federally licensed processor in Thunder Bay, Ontario, manufacturing under two brands of our own — Euphoreal for edibles and Infyn Extracts for extracts — and under contract for other licence holders.

What is the difference between Euphoreal and Infyn Extracts?

Euphoreal is our edibles brand, organised into three collections by dose: Zero at 0 mg THC, Micro at 1 mg of THC per piece and Byte at 5 mg per piece. Infyn Extracts is a separate brand covering standardised oral extract formats, starting with XTRACT, a 9.5 mg THC tablet.

Has the company been making cannabis products for long?

Since 2022. In that time we have produced more than thirty cannabis SKUs for other licence holders and delivered over a million units into Ontario and four other provinces.

Can I buy directly from you?

No. Licensed producers in Canada do not sell recreational cannabis to the public. Our products are distributed through the provincial system and sold by authorised retailers. We do not ship or deliver.

What does “1 mg per piece” mean?

It is the Micro collection’s architecture: each individually moulded piece carries 1 mg of THC, with ten pieces to a pack. The figure is verified by laboratory analysis on every lot.

Which provinces can buy them?

Our own brands are listed with the Ontario Cannabis Store. Products we have manufactured under contract have been sold in Ontario and four other provinces, but that is our manufacturing record rather than a statement about where Euphoreal and Infyn are on shelf today.

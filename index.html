Why it is built this way

What makes the difference

A formulation company first

We develop the format, the extract and the dose ourselves. The product is the output of that work, not the start of it.

Precision at low dose

Both products are built at 1 mg of each cannabinoid per unit. Low doses are harder to make accurately, which is exactly why they are worth engineering properly.

Made in Thunder Bay

Manufactured in Northwestern Ontario in our own federally licensed facility, in small batches.

Information, never inducement

Everything we publish explains how the products are made and what the figures mean. We do not promote consumption.

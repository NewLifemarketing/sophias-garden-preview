Privacy Policy

What this site collects

This is an information site. It does not sell anything, it has no accounts for the general public, and it does not ask visitors for personal information in order to browse it.

The age check

The age gate asks for a province and a date of birth so we can confirm you are of legal age. That check runs entirely in your browser — the date is not transmitted to us and is not stored. Once confirmed, a single value is kept in your browser for the current session only, so you are not asked again on every page. It is cleared when you close the browser.

Forms

Where a page offers a contact or retailer registration form, the information you enter is sent to Sophia’s Garden Artisan so we can respond, and is handled by our customer relationship management provider. Provide only what you are comfortable sending.

The retailer and investor portals

Portal access is for authorized retailers, their staff, and existing investors. Account details are issued and managed by Sophia’s Garden Artisan.

Analytics

If analytics are used on this site they measure aggregate traffic — pages viewed and general location — and are not used to identify individual visitors.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@sophiasartisan.com.

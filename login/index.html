Questions

Common questions

How do I get an access code?

Retailers and budtenders register on the For Retailers page. Accounts on a business domain are approved automatically; anything on a personal domain is reviewed first. Investor codes are issued directly by Sophia’s Garden Artisan.

Is there one login for everyone?

Yes. Retailers, budtenders and investors all use this screen. The code you enter decides which document set you reach.

I am an investor and do not have a code.

Investor access is not available by request or registration. If you are an existing investor and need access or a reset, contact the company directly.

What is behind the login?

Documents only. Everything else on this site — the products, the company, the education material — is open to any adult visitor.

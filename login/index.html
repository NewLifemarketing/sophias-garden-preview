How do I get an access code?

Retailers and budtenders register on the For Retailers page. Accounts on a business domain are approved automatically; anything on a personal domain is reviewed first.

Is there one login for everyone?

Yes. This screen is the single entry point, and the code you enter decides which document set you reach.

I have lost my code.

Email contact@sophiasartisan.com from the address the account was registered with and we will reissue it.

What is behind the login?

Documents only. Everything else on this site — the products, the brands, the company, the education material — is open to any adult visitor.

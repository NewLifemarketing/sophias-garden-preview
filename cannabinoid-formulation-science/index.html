The science behind the format

Cannabinoid formulation science

Formulation is where a cannabinoid stops being an ingredient and becomes a product. This is the part of the business we invest in most.

100%

Lots laboratory tested

Cannabinoids follow fat

Cannabinoids are lipophilic — they associate with fats rather than water. Cocoa butter is a fat, which makes it a natural medium to carry them. Working the extract into the cocoa butter itself, rather than adding it to finished chocolate, is what allows even distribution at low doses.

Why the order of operations matters

A cannabinoid extract added to finished chocolate can appear mixed while remaining unevenly distributed at a smaller scale — and a moulding cavity is a small scale. Incorporating the extract into the fat phase gives the active ingredient a medium it genuinely disperses into rather than one it is merely suspended in. It adds a manufacturing step in order to remove a source of variability, which is usually a good trade.

Working at 1 mg

Low-dose formats are harder to manufacture than high-dose ones. Manufacturing tolerance is proportional, not absolute: an error that is invisible on a 10 mg unit is ten times as significant on a 1 mg one. Precision at 1 mg is a deliberate technical choice, not a limitation.

Working with more than one cannabinoid

THC and CBD are the two most familiar cannabinoids, but they are not the only ones. CBG, used across MICRO ZEST YUZU and carried to 20 mg a piece in ZERO ZEST, brings its own solubility and stability characteristics. Every additional cannabinoid in a formulation is another set of variables to solve for and verify.

The same problem in a different carrier

Everything above describes a fat phase. XTRACT, under our Infyn Extracts brand, has no fat phase at all: it is a standardised uncoated oral tablet carrying 9.5 mg of THC, with no dropper, no measuring step and no hardware. A compressed solid cannot rely on dispersion into cocoa butter, so uniformity has to be won in the blend and in the compression instead. Different carrier, same question: will tablet one thousand carry what tablet one carried?

Why that matters more than the format

A formulation capability that only works in one medium is a recipe. The reason we describe cocoa butter chemistry and tablet compression on the same page is that the discipline underneath them — fix the constraint, build the unit, verify by analysis, lock the process — does not change between them.

Process control gets you to a product you believe is right. Laboratory analysis is what confirms it.

Verification

Every lot is tested by a laboratory licensed for the purpose. The certificate of analysis is the record of what was actually produced rather than what was intended, and it is held on file as part of our lot documentation.

Questions

Common questions

What is a cocoa butter cannabinoid extract?

A cannabinoid incorporated into the cocoa butter phase itself, rather than added to finished chocolate. Cannabinoids are lipophilic, so building them into the fat is what allows even distribution at low doses.

Why not just add distillate to finished chocolate?

Because it can appear mixed while remaining unevenly distributed at a smaller scale — and a moulding cavity is a small scale. Working the extract into the fat phase adds a manufacturing step in order to remove a source of variability.

What is CBG, and why use it?

A cannabinoid distinct from THC and CBD, present in much smaller amounts in most plant material, which is part of why it appears less often in finished products. It carries its own solubility and stability characteristics, so every formulation using it has to be solved again from the beginning.

How does an extract tablet differ from an infused chocolate?

A tablet is a compressed solid with no fat phase to disperse into, so uniformity has to be won in the powder blend and held through compression. Different carrier, same acceptance criterion.

How do you know the formulation worked?

Process control gets you to a product you believe is right; laboratory analysis is what confirms it. Every lot is tested by a laboratory licensed for the purpose, and that record is held on file as part of our lot documentation.

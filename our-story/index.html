Our story

The garden behind the name

Sophia’s Garden (Artisan) Inc. is a Canadian cannabis innovation company based in Thunder Bay, Ontario. We are a manufacturer: a premium edible and extract lab that develops cannabinoid delivery formats and produces them ourselves.

Our approach

What “artisan” means here

Our work sits where confectionery craft meets cannabinoid formulation. We develop cocoa butter cannabinoid extracts, build them into chocolate so that dose is even and stable, and manufacture in small batches at our own facility.

The company

Three years making cannabis products for other companies. Now for ourselves.

Sophia’s Garden (Artisan) Inc. is a federally licensed cannabis processor with three years of production behind us. Over a million units delivered, more than thirty cannabis SKUs manufactured for other licence holders, and two brands of our own: Euphoreal edibles and Infyn Extracts.

Where we started

Built as a contract manufacturer

Sophia’s Garden was built as a contract manufacturer. Since 2022 we have produced more than thirty cannabis SKUs for other licence holders and delivered over a million units into Ontario and four other provinces. That work is still part of the business.

Why we launched our own brands

You learn what a category is missing by making everyone else’s product

You learn what a category is missing by making everyone else’s product. We saw an edible assortment concentrated between 2 mg and 10 mg of THC per piece, dominated by gummies, with almost nothing built for a consumer who wants a smaller and more predictable increment. Euphoreal is the answer to that. Infyn Extracts is the answer to another question on the extract side.

Our approach

How we work

Our work sits where confectionery craft meets cannabinoid formulation. We develop cocoa butter cannabinoid extracts, build them into chocolate so that dose is even and stable, and manufacture in small batches at our own facility. The extract side runs on the same discipline in a different carrier.

The team

Formulation, production and quality in one building

Formulation, production and quality sit in the same building. Our chocolate work is led by an award-winning chocolatier, and every lot is released by a quality assurance person before it ships.

Questions

Common questions

Does Sophia’s Garden Artisan grow cannabis?

No. We are a processor. We formulate and manufacture finished cannabis products; we do not cultivate.

How long has the company been manufacturing?

Since 2022. Over that period we have produced more than thirty cannabis SKUs, delivered over a million units, and supplied into Ontario and four other provinces.

Do you make products for other companies?

Yes, and it is how the company was built. Contract manufacturing for other licence holders is still a working part of the business, covering formulation development, production, packaging and quality release.

Where is the company based?

In Thunder Bay, Ontario. Formulation, production and quality all sit in the same building.

Is the company federally licensed?

Yes, as a federally licensed processor. Health Canada publishes the list of licence holders.

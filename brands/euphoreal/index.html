Blissful Magic

Three collections separated by dose, not by flavour.

Euphoreal is built as a ladder. Zero carries no THC. Micro is 1 mg of THC per piece. Byte is 5 mg per piece. A consumer, a budtender or a buyer can place any product in the range in one sentence.

Questions

Common questions

What is Euphoreal?

Our edibles brand. It is organised as a dose ladder in three collections — Zero, Micro and Byte — so that the first thing you learn about any product in it is how much THC is in one piece.

Why separate Zero into its own collection?

Because its defining promise is 0 mg of THC, which is a different proposition rather than a smaller version of Micro. A 1 mg microdose is still THC. Zero is for the consumer who is specifically choosing a product without it.

Are all Euphoreal products chocolate?

So far, yes. Chocolate is where our deepest manufacturing capability sits. The brand is defined by dose architecture rather than by format, so that is not a permanent constraint.

Which Euphoreal products can I buy today?

MICRO ZEN MILK and MICRO ZEST YUZU are approved by the OCS and are pre-launch. The rest of the range is in development. Each SKU on this site carries its own status label.

Questions

Common questions

What is Infyn Extracts?

Our extracts brand, and a separate brand from Euphoreal. It covers standardised oral formats — solid units carrying a fixed cannabinoid content with no measuring step and no hardware.

What is XTRACT?

A precision-dosed uncoated cannabis tablet carrying 9.5 mg of THC, with fifty tablets to a jar. It sits in the capsules category, not edibles, and is currently in development.

How is an extract tablet different from an infused chocolate?

Both are swallowed, so they take the same route through the body. The carrier is what differs: a chocolate disperses its cannabinoid into cocoa butter, while a tablet is a compressed solid with no fat phase, so uniformity is achieved in the blend and the compression instead.

Why a second brand rather than another Euphoreal collection?

Because it is a different category, a different consumer and a different shelf. Euphoreal’s ladder is built around edible dose architecture, and a 9.5 mg tablet does not belong on it.

Questions

Common questions

Do you deliver to these areas?

No. The areas listed are where our products are distributed to authorised retailers through the provincial system. We do not sell to the public or ship anywhere.

Why list cities if you do not sell directly?

So an adult in that area can see what we make, and so a retailer there can find the trade documentation. These pages are information, not an offer of sale.

Is the product range the same across Ontario?

What we supply to the provincial system is identical province-wide. Which stores choose to range it is each retailer’s decision.

How do I find a store near me that carries a product?

Use the product locator on the Where to Find Us page. It reads live retailer menu data, so it reflects what is actually on a shelf rather than what we hope is.

Northwestern Ontario

Distribution across the region

Northwestern Ontario is the hardest part of the province to serve and the part we know best, because we are in it. Distances between centres here are measured in hours rather than minutes, retail is concentrated in a small number of towns, and a single store often serves a catchment larger than some southern counties. Product still moves through the same provincial system as everywhere else in Ontario — the difference is the ground it has to cover.

Questions

Thunder Bay — common questions

Does being close to Thunder Bay mean faster restocking?

Not directly. Every order in Ontario flows through the provincial system regardless of how near a store is to the producer, so proximity to our facility does not change how a retailer orders or how quickly stock arrives.

Where can I buy Sophia’s Garden Artisan in Thunder Bay?

Through authorized cannabis retailers in Thunder Bay. Like every licensed producer in Ontario, we supply the provincial system and stores order from it — we do not sell to the public directly, and we do not ship or deliver.

Which products are available in Thunder Bay?

MICRO ZEN, a balanced THC:CBD milk chocolate, and MICRO ZEST, a balanced THC:CBG yuzu chocolate. Both are formulated at 1 mg of each cannabinoid per unit, ten units to a pack. Whether a particular store carries them is that retailer’s decision.

Are the products made in Thunder Bay?

Yes. Our facility is at 600 Montreal St. in Thunder Bay, where the formulation and the manufacturing are both done.

I run a store in Thunder Bay. How do I stock them?

Register for a retailer account with your work email and order through the provincial catalogue. The portal carries sell sheets, spec sheets, certificates of analysis and point-of-sale material.

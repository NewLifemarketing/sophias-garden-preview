Questions

Mississauga — common questions

How does a store decide to range a smaller producer here?

Usually on a mix of category fit, documentation and how easily staff can explain the product. Our specification sheets and product comparison charts are available to registered retailers for exactly that reason.

I run a store in Mississauga. How do I stock them?

Register for a retailer account with your work email and order through the provincial catalogue. The portal carries sell sheets, specification sheets, comparison charts and point-of-sale material.

What does 1 mg per piece mean in practice?

In the Euphoreal Micro collection each individually moulded piece carries 1 mg of THC, with ten pieces in a pack. It is a low, measured format, and the figure is verified by laboratory analysis on every lot.

Why is Mississauga listed if you do not sell here directly?

Because our products are distributed across Ontario and are available to retailers in Mississauga through the provincial catalogue. This page explains what we make and how it reaches the shelf — it is information, not an offer of sale.

Is any of this available outside Ontario?

Our own brands are listed with the Ontario Cannabis Store. Products we have manufactured under contract have been sold in five provinces, but we do not announce a province for our own brands until we have signed listings there.

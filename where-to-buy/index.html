Questions

Common questions

Where can I buy these products?

Only through authorised cannabis retailers. Use the product locator on this page to see which stores are carrying a given product right now — it reads live retailer menu data, so it is the accurate answer rather than a promise.

Can I order from this website?

No. We are a manufacturer and do not sell to the public. Ontario retailers order through the OCS catalogue.

My local store does not stock them, what can I do?

Check the locator first — it will show the nearest store that does. Ranging is each retailer’s own decision, and a store that does not carry a product has usually chosen not to range it rather than been unable to.

When will other provinces get them?

We are not announcing provinces without signed listings. Products we have manufactured under contract have been sold in five provinces, but that is a different thing from our own brands being listed there.

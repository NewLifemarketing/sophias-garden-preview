About this product

What it is

XTRACT is the first product under Infyn Extracts and the clearest proof that the formulation discipline is not specific to chocolate. It is a standardised uncoated oral tablet carrying 9.5 mg of THC, with fifty tablets to a jar. There is no dropper, no measuring step and no device: the unit is the dose.

A tablet and a chocolate piece pose the same question — will unit one thousand carry what unit one carried — in completely different carriers. A compressed solid has no fat phase to disperse into, so uniformity is solved in the blend and the compression rather than in the temper.

50 tablets to a pack, each formulated at 9.5 mg THC per tablet.

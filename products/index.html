Questions

Common questions

What is the difference between the Zero, Micro and Byte collections?

Dose, and nothing else about the naming. Zero carries no THC at all. Micro is 1 mg of THC per piece. Byte is 5 mg per piece. That ladder is deliberate: any product in the range can be placed in one sentence.

What is the difference between MICRO ZEN and MICRO ZEST?

The second cannabinoid and the flavour system built around it. MICRO ZEN is a milk chocolate pairing THC with CBD. MICRO ZEST is a yuzu chocolate pairing THC with CBG. Both are balanced at 1 mg of each cannabinoid per piece.

How much is in a package?

It depends on the collection. Health Canada caps an edible package at 10 mg of THC. Micro divides that into ten pieces of 1 mg; Byte divides the same cap into two pieces of 5 mg. Zero carries no THC, and ZERO ZEST holds 400 mg of CBG across twenty pieces. XTRACT is a tablet rather than an edible, at 9.5 mg of THC across fifty tablets.

Why is there no price on this site?

Pricing for cannabis products is set by the provincial distributors, not by us, so it would be inaccurate for us to publish it.

Is the range going to grow?

Yes. MICRO ZEN and MICRO ZEST were approved in OCS Product Call 4. Further SKUs across both brands are in development, and each is labelled on this site with its actual status rather than presented as available.

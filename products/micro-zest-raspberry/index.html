About this product

What it is

MICRO ZEST RASPBERRY carries the Micro architecture into a familiar fruit flavour and a materially different cannabinoid ratio. One piece remains 1 mg of THC. The paired cannabinoid rises to 5 mg of CBG per piece, or 50 mg across the ten-piece package.

Raspberry is a more familiar flavour than yuzu and a harder one to build without leaning on sugar. The formulation target was flavour identity at the same sweetness level as the rest of the collection.

This SKU extends MICRO ZEST, approved by the OCS in Product Call 4.

10 pieces to a pack, each formulated at 1 mg THC / 5 mg CBG per piece.

Where it sits

Euphoreal — the Micro collection

One piece equals 1 mg of THC. Ten pieces to a package. The architecture does not change across the collection — what changes is the flavour and the paired cannabinoid ratio.

Easy to understand for a consumer, easy to explain for a budtender, easy to step up gradually, easy to remember at shelf.

Questions

Common questions

What is in MICRO ZEST RASPBERRY?

1 mg THC : 5 mg CBG Raspberry Chocolate — 1 mg THC / 5 mg CBG per piece, with 10 pieces to a pack. Per package that is total thc 10 mg · total cbg 50 mg per package.

How is it different from MICRO ZEN?

MICRO ZEN is balanced thc:cbd milk chocolate at 1 mg THC / 1 mg CBD per piece, in the Micro collection. MICRO ZEST RASPBERRY sits in Micro, at 1 mg THC per piece. The collection tells you the dose architecture; the SKU tells you the flavour and the paired cannabinoid.

Where can I buy MICRO ZEST RASPBERRY?

Our products are sold only through authorised cannabis retailers — use the product locator on the Where to Find Us page to see which stores are carrying it right now. We do not sell to the public or ship directly.

Is the potency on the package accurate?

Yes. Cannabinoid content is verified by laboratory analysis on every lot before it is released, and a quality assurance person signs that release.

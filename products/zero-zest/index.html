About this product

What it is

ZERO ZEST is the first product in the Euphoreal Zero collection, and the collection exists because of a specific request. Regional Ontario retailers asked for a zero-THC, high-CBG format, and Micro could not answer it: a 1 mg microdose is still THC. Zero Zest carries no THC at all and 20 mg of CBG in each of twenty matcha chocolate pieces.

Twenty pieces rather than ten is a different pack architecture to Micro, not a variation on it. Without a THC ceiling to divide, the portioning logic is set by the CBG loading instead.

20 pieces to a pack, each formulated at 0 mg THC / 20 mg CBG per piece.

Questions

Common questions

What is in ZERO ZEST?

CBG Matcha Chocolate — 0 mg THC / 20 mg CBG per piece, with 20 pieces to a pack. Per package that is total thc 0 mg · total cbg 400 mg per package.

How is it different from MICRO ZEN?

MICRO ZEN is balanced thc:cbd milk chocolate at 1 mg THC / 1 mg CBD per piece, in the Micro collection. ZERO ZEST sits in Zero, at 0 mg THC. The collection tells you the dose architecture; the SKU tells you the flavour and the paired cannabinoid.

Where can I buy ZERO ZEST?

Our products are sold only through authorised cannabis retailers — use the product locator on the Where to Find Us page to see which stores are carrying it right now. We do not sell to the public or ship directly.

Is the potency on the package accurate?

Yes. Cannabinoid content is verified by laboratory analysis on every lot before it is released, and a quality assurance person signs that release.

About this product

What it is

MICRO ZEST pairs THC with CBG — a less common cannabinoid than CBD — in a yuzu chocolate at 1 mg of each per unit. Yuzu is a Japanese citrus with a sharp, aromatic profile that stands up to chocolate, and it was selected as a formulation decision: the citrus carries the flavour without additional sweetness.

Working with a second cannabinoid means a second set of formulation variables — solubility, stability and even distribution all have to be solved again.

Ten units to a pack, each formulated at 1 mg THC / 1 mg CBG per unit.

About this product

What it is

MICRO ZEN is a milk chocolate formulated to deliver a precise, balanced dose of two cannabinoids: 1 mg of THC and 1 mg of CBD in every piece. The format exists because dose accuracy in an edible is a manufacturing problem before it is anything else — cannabinoids have to be distributed evenly through a fat-based medium and stay that way through tempering, moulding and setting.

Built on our cocoa butter cannabinoid extract work, which lets the active ingredients disperse into the chocolate rather than sit alongside it.

MICRO ZEN DARK, a higher-CBD dark chocolate extension of this platform, is in development.

10 pieces to a pack, each formulated at 1 mg THC / 1 mg CBD per piece.

Where it sits

Euphoreal — the Micro collection

One piece equals 1 mg of THC. Ten pieces to a package. The architecture does not change across the collection — what changes is the flavour and the paired cannabinoid ratio.

Easy to understand for a consumer, easy to explain for a budtender, easy to step up gradually, easy to remember at shelf.

Questions

Common questions

What is in MICRO ZEN?

Balanced THC:CBD Milk Chocolate — 1 mg THC / 1 mg CBD per piece, with 10 pieces to a pack. Per package that is total thc 10 mg · total cbd 10 mg per package.

How is it different from MICRO ZEST?

MICRO ZEST is balanced thc:cbg yuzu chocolate at 1 mg THC / 1 mg CBG per piece, in the Micro collection. MICRO ZEN sits in Micro, at 1 mg THC per piece. The collection tells you the dose architecture; the SKU tells you the flavour and the paired cannabinoid.

Where can I buy MICRO ZEN?

Approved in OCS Product Call 4. Pre-launch — no purchase order yet. Our products are sold only through authorised cannabis retailers — use the product locator on the Where to Find Us page to see which stores are carrying it right now. We do not sell to the public or ship directly.

Is the potency on the package accurate?

Yes. Cannabinoid content is verified by laboratory analysis on every lot before it is released, and a quality assurance person signs that release.

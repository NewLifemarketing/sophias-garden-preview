Questions

Common questions

How do I open a retail account?

Register on this page with a company email address. Accounts on a business domain are approved automatically; anything else is reviewed by us.

How do I actually order stock?

Through your provincial distributor. Ontario retailers order via the OCS catalogue. This site handles enquiries and documentation only — it does not take orders, and pricing is set by the distributor rather than by us.

What is the difference between a retailer and a budtender account?

Retailer accounts carry point-of-sale materials, product comparison charts and the current product documentation. Budtender accounts carry the education kit — product knowledge guides, cannabinoid education cards, the dose-ladder explainer and counter cheat sheets.

Do you offer incentives for stocking or recommending the products?

No. Ontario’s rule is education only, with no financial incentives of any kind, and we hold to that.

Have you manufactured for other licence holders?

Yes. More than thirty cannabis SKUs since 2022, with over a million units produced and delivered. Your listing would not be our first.
